How to generate a CAMS statement

  1. Go to MF Central (mfcentral.com) or CAMS (camsonline.com) → Statements → Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) — CAMS + KFintech.
  2. Enter your PAN and registered email, pick the period (choose Since Inception for everything, or a date range) and the Detailed statement, then submit.
  3. Verify with the OTP sent to your registered email/mobile, and set or note the password for the PDF (often your PAN in CAPITALS).
  4. Within a few minutes a password-protected PDF arrives in your email.
  5. Come back here and upload it above with that password.

Note: a CAMS statement covers mutual funds only. Direct stocks, bonds and SGBs come from your NSDL CAS (via Upload CAS).
