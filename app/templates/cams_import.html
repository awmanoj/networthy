Doing it by hand

  1. Open CAMS → Statements → CAS – CAMS + KFintech (direct link).
  2. Statement type: Summary (balances & valuation) is enough; Detailed also works. Leave the as-on date as today.
  3. Enter your registered email (the one your folios are registered under — the PDF is mailed only there). PAN is optional. Set Password and Confirm Password to your PAN in CAPITALS.
  4. Press Submit (complete an OTP if asked). The password-protected PDF arrives in your email within a few minutes.
  5. Come back and upload it below.
