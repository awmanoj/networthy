Where a spreadsheet stops working

Honestly: for a year or two, it doesn't stop. A spreadsheet is free, it's yours, it works offline, and nobody can shut it down. If that's what you want, take the file above and ignore the rest of this page.

What eventually goes wrong is always the same three things:

  • Every figure is stale the moment you type it. Your equity and mutual funds move every day. A quarterly snapshot of a live portfolio is a photograph of a river — fine for the trend, useless for "what am I worth right now".
  • Looking up the numbers is the actual work. Not the typing — the hunting. Opening the demat account, the CAMS statement, three bank apps and the EPFO portal is an hour, and it's the same hour every quarter. That's the thing people stop doing, and a tracker nobody updates is worse than none.
  • It can't tell you anything you didn't already put in. Where you rank, what you'd need to retire, what a 3% withdrawal rate implies, how a goal is tracking — each is a formula you'd have to build and maintain yourself.

Networthy HQ is the same table with the lookups automated: import an NSDL CAS and your demat and mutual-fund holdings appear priced live; add property, gold, PPF and loans once. It's free, and you can export everything back to CSV whenever you want — including into this spreadsheet.
