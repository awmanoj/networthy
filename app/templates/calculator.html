The net worth formula

Net worth = Assets − Liabilities

That's the whole of it. Every disagreement about someone's net worth is a disagreement about what goes on each side and what it's worth today — never about the arithmetic.

Four things that make the number wrong

  1. Subtracting the same loan twice. Entering a flat at "value minus the home loan" and then listing the home loan again is the most common error, and it can knock tens of lakhs off a real net worth. Count the property gross; subtract the loan once, on the other side.
  2. Using what you paid instead of what it's worth. Purchase price is history. Property, gold and equity all count at today's value — that's the entire point of the number.
  3. Counting the EMI as the debt. What you owe is the outstanding balance, not the monthly instalment and not the amount you originally borrowed.
  4. Leaving out EPF, PPF and jewellery. Illiquid isn't the same as not owned. For most salaried people in India the retirement balances alone are a large share of the total, and they're the most commonly forgotten line.
