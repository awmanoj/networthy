About Networthy

Networthy is a personal net-worth tracker built for people who want one honest, up-to-date answer to a simple question: what am I actually worth?

Most of us hold wealth in a dozen scattered places — a demat account, a couple of mutual-fund folios, some US stocks, a bit of crypto, gold in a locker, a flat, a PPF, a home loan. No single app adds it all up. Networthy does: it imports your NSDL CAS and your CAMS mutual-fund statement, lets you add everything else by hand, prices what it can live, and rolls it into a single number — assets minus liabilities.

What it covers

Indian and US equities, mutual funds, crypto, gold & silver, bonds and other fixed income (PPF, EPF, NPS, FDs), real estate, private business, foreign currency, bank balances and cash — and, on the other side of the ledger, every kind of loan. It also helps you look ahead: your recurring expenses, your monthly burn, and how close you are to a FIRE (financially independent) number.

Live, not last month's

Your holdings are valued in real time. Indian & US stocks and crypto are priced live from public markets, and mutual-fund NAVs refresh from AMFI's official daily feed — so your net worth reflects where things stand today, not whatever your last statement said.

Import in seconds

Upload an NSDL CAS and your demat & mutual-fund holdings appear at once; import a CAMS statement to pull in mutual funds across every AMC. Everything else — property, gold, foreign accounts, loans — takes a few taps by hand, and updates roll straight into your total.

Built India-first

Amounts are in rupees with lakh/crore grouping, holdings import straight from an NSDL CAS, and the "where do you stand" view is modelled on Indian wealth data (alongside a few other geographies) so the comparison actually means something.

Private by principle

Your financial data never leaves the server it runs on. The one deliberate exception is fetching live prices, where the only thing sent out is a public ticker or currency symbol — never your holdings, amounts, or identity. See our Privacy page for the details.
